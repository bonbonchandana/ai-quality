Use your own dataset

Upload an hourly CSV (or ZIP) with a time column (Date + Time, year/month/day/hour or datetime) and pollutant columns such as CO, NO2, NOx, C6H6, PM2.5, PM10, SO2 or O3. Weather columns (temperature, humidity, pressure, wind…) are used when present. Example: the UCI Beijing station files.

How it works

  1. Data: hourly readings. In the UCI file, -200 means the value is missing.
  2. Lagged features: the pollutant 1, 2, 3 and 24 hours earlier, plus the previous hour's weather.
  3. Model: it predicts how much the level will go up or down in the next hour and adds that to the last reading.
  4. Testing on the future: the first 60% of the timeline is for training, the next 20% for picking the model, and the last 20% for the final test.
  5. Flagging: a test hour is flagged when the miss is bigger than 95% of the misses on the validation period.
  6. Analysis: if some inputs were missing or the data had a gap just before, the episode is a data limitation. If not, it is treated as unusual pollution.
