How it works

  1. Data: hourly readings; the value -200 means missing.
  2. Lagged features: the pollutant 1, 2, 3 and 24 hours earlier, plus the previous hour's temperature and humidity.
  3. Future-period evaluation: train on the first 60% of the timeline, pick the best model on the next 20%, test on the last 20%.
  4. Flagging: a test hour is flagged when |actual − predicted| is larger than the 95th percentile of validation errors.
  5. Analysis: if inputs were missing or the data had a gap just before, it is a data limitation; otherwise an unusual pollution event.
