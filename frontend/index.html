Flagged episodes

An episode is a run of hours where the real value was much further from the prediction than usual. Each one is checked in this order:

  • Data limitation: inputs were missing, the data had a gap just before, the reading was stuck on one value, or it jumped suddenly while nothing else moved.
  • Unusual pollution: the data looked fine and other pollutants rose or fell at the same time. They are measured by separate instruments, so this points to a real event.
  • Unclear: the data looked fine but nothing else moved with it. It may be a small local event, or the model may simply have missed a normal change.

    Use your own data

    Upload an hourly CSV, Excel or ZIP file. It needs a time column (Date and Time, year/month/day/hour, or datetime) and at least one pollutant column such as CO, NO2, NOx, C6H6, PM2.5, PM10, SO2 or O3. Any weather columns in the file are used too. The UCI Beijing station files work as they are.
